MISSION BRIEFING

How to Survive on Mars

🎯 YOUR GOAL

Run Jezero Base for 20 sols (a sol is one Mars day). Collect 80 Science to win. You lose if O₂, H₂O or Food reaches 0%, or the crew's radiation dose reaches 100%.

Everything you do costs power (kW), and you only get a limited amount each sol. So the real game is choosing what to power and what to leave off.

🔁 HOW A SOL (DAY) WORKS
  1. Read the forecast in the bottom bar. It tells you if a solar flare or dust storm is coming.
  2. Click a building (or press E next to it) to walk inside. Press the glowing wall buttons to act. Each button can be used once per sol.
  3. Collect scrap and explore if you like (see below).
  4. Press Advance to Sol…. Your supplies drain, new power arrives, a new forecast appears and a surprise event may happen.
📊 WHAT EACH NUMBER MEANS
O₂ (oxygen)Air for the crew. Drops about 6–8% each sol. At 0% the mission ends. Fix it with MOXIE in the Habitat.
H₂O (water)Drinking and farming water. Drops about 4–6% each sol. At 0% the mission ends. Clean water in the Habitat.
FOODCrew meals. Drops about 3–5% each sol. At 0% the mission ends. Harvest crops in the Food Silo.
DOSE (radiation)Radiation the crew has absorbed. It only goes UP (lower is better). +1% every sol, plus a big jump on solar-flare days unless you shelter. At 100% the crew must go home. Above 60% your science results are halved.
POWER (kW)Your budget for today. It refills each sol: about 6–10 kW when panels are open and clean, only 5 when they are stowed or in a dust storm, minus standby costs.
BATT (battery)Stored power. Charge it on a good day (costs 3 kW for +2) and use it on a bad day.
NEXT SOLAn estimate of how much power you will get tomorrow after modules that are ON use their standby power.
SCIENCEYour score. Reach the goal to win. Earn it with the spectrometer, comms uplinks, the rover and exploring.
SCRAPSalvaged metal. Pay for upgrades and special builds (radiation shield, extra solar panels, a drill, a CubeSat…).
Module health barsEach building has a hidden condition (crew morale, panel cleanliness, instrument calibration, crop health, antenna alignment). They fade each sol and weaken that module's results below 40%. Maintenance buttons restore them.
🏠 THE FIVE MODULES + ON/OFF SWITCHES

🏠 Habitat: oxygen, water, storm shelter, movie night. · ☀️ Solar Array: power, battery, dust brushing. · 🔬 Research Lab: science and calibration. · 🌱 Food Silo: crops and spare parts. · 📡 Comms Dish: send science to Earth.

Every module except solar has a POWER switch on its left wall. ON uses standby power each sol (Habitat 2, Lab 2, Silo 1, Comms 1 kW) but gives small free gains by itself. OFF saves that power but locks the module's buttons (except the storm shelter). Floating labels over each building show its ON/OFF state.

🔩 SCRAP, HILLS & THE ROVER

Each sol, 1–3 pieces of scrap appear as glowing amber markers on the map. Click one to claim it (in first person, aim at it within about 9 m). The minimap shows them as yellow squares.

The cyan beacons on the far hills are exploration sites (cyan diamonds on the minimap). Click one to send the rover (2 kW). It drives there and comes back after 2–4 sols with science. The first visit pays the most. Or walk there in first person and survey on foot for +2 science (adds a little radiation).

⚠️ FORECAST & SURPRISES

Solar flare: stay safe by pressing Activate Storm Shelter in the Habitat before advancing. Skip it and you take 3.5 times more radiation. Dust storm: stow the panels first, or they get scratched (max power drops permanently). Surprise events pop up after a sol and make you pick between two costly choices.

Data sources: archived solar flare dates from NASA DONKI (not live); radiation from Curiosity RAD; dust storms, ice, soil and relay facts from NASA Phoenix, Opportunity, InSight, Ingenuity and Perseverance mission archives.
