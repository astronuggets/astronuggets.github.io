02 // Mission Objective

Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer

The Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer is a deep-space habitat simulation designed to prepare future astronauts for off-world survival. Each turn represents one Martian day—known as a Sol—where every command carries a critical trade-off. From limited electrical power to shrinking water reserves, your resources deplete every single day, forcing you to balance crew survival, radiation defense, and scientific progress.

1. Sustain Life Support

Monitor O₂ and H₂O reserves inside the Habitation Module each Sol. Keep Life Support above 0% to ensure crew survival.

2. Balance the Power Grid

Route limited kilowatts from Solar Arrays and RTG nuclear batteries across Life Support, Shielding, and Research.

3. Survive Space Weather

Deflect incoming solar storms with magnetic shielding and retract solar panels before Martian dust storms hit.

4. Advance Space Science

Allocate spare energy to the Research Lab and Comms Array (with 3–22 min light-speed delay) to hit mission science goals.
